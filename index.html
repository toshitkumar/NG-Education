Arduino Basics & Electronics

Complete electronics fundamentals, Ohm's law, breadboard prototyping, hardware safety, and Arduino Uno board architecture.

Hardware & Tools Required
Microcontroller Board Arduino Uno R3: Microcontroller board for understanding hardware architecture and power connections.
Prototyping Kit Breadboard & Jumper Wires: For assembling and testing circuits without soldering.
Core Components Electronic Components: Resistors, capacitors, LEDs, diodes, transistors and push buttons.
Testing Equipment Digital Multimeter: For measuring voltage, resistance and continuity safely.
Course Completion Outcomes
  • Explain fundamental electrical quantities and apply Ohm’s Law.
  • Read basic circuit diagrams and assemble breadboard circuits.
  • Identify resistors, capacitors, LEDs, diodes and transistors.
  • Understand Arduino Uno pins, onboard components and power regulation.
  • Use a multimeter to check connections and measure circuit parameters.
  • Identify common hardware faults and follow safe prototyping practices.
