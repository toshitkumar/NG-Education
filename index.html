Arduino Motor Control

Master DC motor drivers, PWM speed regulation, precision servo positioning, and stepper motor motion calibration for robotics and automation.

Course Specifications & Overview
Skill Level Intermediate
Total Modules 12 Modules
Recommended Duration 15–20 hours
Learning Format Hands-on Motor & Actuator Projects
Prerequisites Arduino programming, digital I/O and basic electronics knowledge are recommended.
Hardware & Software Requirements
Controller Arduino Uno R3, USB cable
DC Motor Control DC geared motors, L298N or L293D driver
Servo Control SG90 micro-servo or compatible positional servo
Stepper Control Compatible stepper motor and driver module
Sensory Inputs Potentiometer, push buttons, optional sensors
Safety & Assembly External power supply, breadboard, jumper wires & connectors
Software Arduino IDE, Servo library and appropriate stepper-control library
Course Completion Outcomes
  • Explain DC, servo and stepper motor operating principles.
  • Control DC motor direction and speed using suitable drivers and PWM.
  • Position servo motors using programmed angles.
  • Calculate step counts and control stepper motor rotation.
  • Integrate sensors with motor-control logic.
  • Design and troubleshoot motorized robotics and automation prototypes.
⚠️ Important Technical Note: L298N and L293D drivers have different current and thermal limits, so the selected motor must be compatible with the driver. A stepper motor must use a suitable driver and power supply. Never power motors directly from Arduino GPIO pins.
